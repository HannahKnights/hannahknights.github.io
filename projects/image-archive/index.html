Image archive

Ongoing / Indefinite

About

Photography and image archiving has been a continued part of my practice since the beginning. This collection is not medium specific and includes film photography, moving image, smartphone images, scans and screenshots.

Often images are pushed back and forth between these mediums (re-photographed, scanned, digitsed, re-printed), these interactions between material and digital processes become a subject in and of themselves. Images from within the archive are often revisited at different points over time, re-evaluated and cast in new lights.Recurring motifs across these everyday subjects include: reflections (glass, mirrors) and light, nature, plays on depth and focus, frames (fences, window).

This collection and practice accompanies other parts of my work, often used as a tool for thinking, of experimentation or as another medium for reflection.
